## Perfect AI study method

All 2,702 SKILL.md files were read programmatically in full and recorded by relative path, SHA-256, family, metadata and paragraph identity. Shared content was consolidated; per-skill purpose/constraint cards and mechanism/context matrices were used for review, with detailed source/tool inspection by family. The separate research ledger records this method explicitly. This is a machine-assisted corpus study, not a claim of an independent manual line-by-line audit of 1.18 million words, nor execution of all 2,702 capabilities.

The 576 mechanism/context skills and 680 framework/context skills are tracked individually but do not become 1,256 runtime modules. Their concepts map into a smaller task-oriented taxonomy. Classification is heuristic and reviewable; unmapped or ambiguous details are not silently treated as verified design rules. Raw upstream skill text, catalogs, fonts, models and assets are not redistributed in this package.

## Provenance and licensing

The package's instructions, helpers and demonstrations were newly written for this project. Source influence is disclosed above; original source code, datasets and substantial source passages are not bundled. The 1.2.0 additions were rewritten from the concepts of the session-bundled skills named above; no passages were copied. Perfect AI includes mixed-origin material and an origin/license document, so no blanket permission is assumed for its upstream assets. The Ponytail decision ladder is intentionally adapted at the conceptual/workflow level; its MIT notice and the Pro Max MIT notice are retained in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`. An Apache 2.0 license copy for the inspected Anthropic material is included conservatively in `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt`; it does not imply affiliation or that the whole package is an Anthropic derivative.

No live model A/B study, independent designer rating, real user study or universal token/quality improvement was measured. Unit tests, source inspection, local browser checks and self-authored examples each provide narrower evidence described in `BUILD-REPORT.md`.
