Media notification, lock screen and widget

On Android 13 and newer the media card in the shade and on the lock screen is drawn by the system (SystemUI, or One UI on Samsung). The app cannot restyle it or replace its progress bar. What the app does control decides how good it looks: sharp square artwork, complete metadata, the play state, and two custom buttons. The card's color and its squiggly progress bar come from our artwork automatically. The Melodiha look on Samsung is One UI rendering the same data.
