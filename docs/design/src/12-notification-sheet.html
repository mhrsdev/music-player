Media notification, lock screen and widget

On Android 13+ the media card in the shade and on the lock screen is drawn by the system (SystemUI, or One UI on Samsung): the app cannot restyle it. What we control decides how good it looks: sharp square artwork, complete metadata, the play state, two custom buttons, and our own monochrome icon (the arch with bars). The home-screen widget is fully ours and speaks the app's language: artwork light, discs and a loudness-bars progress.
